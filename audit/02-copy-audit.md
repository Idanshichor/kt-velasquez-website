# Copy audit (recommendations only)

Date: 2026-10-18. The copy stays word for word in the rebuild. Nothing here is applied unless Idan says yes to a specific line. Rules used: `about-me/writing-rules.md` (no em dashes, no AI tells, be specific).

## What works

- The voice. "Sin Pelos en la Lengua" is direct and has a real point of view.
- "Soy la estratega a la que llamas cuando estás listo para dejar de adivinar" is a strong line. Specific, human.
- The About preview says what makes her different (civil engineer, two master's, structure plus communication plus mindset) in plain words.

## Recommendations

### A. Em dashes (house rule says none anywhere)

| Where | Text | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|
| Hero, hidden H1 text | `— {h1_keyword}` | Use a comma or a space. Not visible to people, but search engines read it. |
| Service button | `Pagar Programa — $price` | `Pagar Programa · $price` or `Pagar Programa: $price` |
| EN hero description | "scale with clarity—without burning out" | "scale with clarity, without burning out" |
| EN about chapters (2 lines) | "...sad my story was—there were also..." | Comma or period |
| Image alt text (podcast, sobre-mi) | "Sin Pelos en la Lengua — Podcast..." | Comma or colon |
| Page title `/sobre-mi` | "Quién es Katherine Velasquez — Mi Historia" | "Quién es Katherine Velasquez: Mi Historia" |

Impact: tiny. Risk: none.

### B. Specificity

1. **Hero description** ("escalar con claridad", "estructura de negocio, estrategia digital y mentalidad ejecutiva") uses general consulting words. Stronger: say who and what result. Example direction (not final): name the client type and one outcome. Needs Katherine's input on real outcomes.
2. **Banner description** is one very long sentence with a grammar slip ("líderes que construyen su marca personal que ya pasaron la etapa de improvisar"). Suggest a rewrite, two sentences. Katherine to approve.
3. **Only one testimonial has a number** ($250,000 in sales). Ask Katherine for one more real result (clients, time, revenue range) to put next to the other two. No invented numbers.
4. **Trust strip label** "Confían en Katherine" has no result attached. Optional: one line under the logos with a real count of clients served, only if true.

### C. CTA consistency

At least three labels for the same WhatsApp action: "Hablemos de tu proyecto" (hero), "CONVERSA CONMIGO" (diagnostic), and a third on the final CTA. Pick one label for the WhatsApp action and use it everywhere (recommended: "Hablemos de tu proyecto"). Payment CTAs stay separate ("Pagar Programa", "Contratar Acompañamiento").

### D. Services section

- Section is dense. The text itself is fine, but feature bullets are long. Suggest an optional "short" line per tier (one sentence) shown above the full list. Only with approval.
- "Más Popular" badge is derived by checking if the plan name contains the word "Popular". If the name changes, the badge disappears. Code risk, not copy, noted for the rebuild.

### E. Language

- English is a toggle, not a page. Translation quality is fine, but see the SEO audit: Google cannot index it.
- Mixed register: hero uses "tu", testimonial in EN uses "she". Fine, just keep it consistent when new copy is added.

### F. Testimonials

- The Hilit Hochman quote is English-only and short. In the Spanish view it stays in English. Offer: show it with a small "(en inglés)" tag, or ask Hilit's permission for a Spanish version. Idan's call.
- Pnina Shichor (Bound to Travel) shares a surname with Idan. If she is family, some readers will discount the quote. Idan's call whether to keep it as the lead quote.
